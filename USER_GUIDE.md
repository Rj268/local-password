# Local Password — quick guide

## First time

1. Open Local Password.
2. Skim or skip the short welcome.
3. Open **Create**, choose **Generate password**, then **Save to Vault** and give it a name.
4. Choose a passphrase (at least 8 characters). Write down the recovery key shown once.

## Every day

- **Create** — Characters or Words. Use the length slider and switches to choose options. Use **Also exclude** when a site rejects certain symbols. Passwords stay masked until you choose **Show**. **Number of passwords** lets you generate a batch.
- **Vault** — Search and filter saved accounts. Choose **View details** to show account details and password actions. Use the star to mark a favorite. Extra actions such as archive, rename, notes, and history are under **Manage**.
- **Lock** — Hides the vault until you unlock with the passphrase or recovery key.
- **Settings → Lock on open** — Optional. When on, the app asks for the passphrase as soon as it starts.
- **Vault → Check breaches** — Optional. Asks a public leak list whether a password appeared in known breaches. Only a short hash prefix is sent — not the password or vault. The password-health summary above search is calculated locally.

## Backup

- **Settings → Export vault** copies the encrypted file. Keep it private.
- **Import vault** replaces the vault on this computer after a warning.
- Send / Receive vault moves that encrypted file to another device on the same Wi-Fi. The passphrase is not sent.

## Privacy

Nothing is uploaded for analytics or advertising. Passwords stay on this computer. See `PRIVACY.md` and `SECURITY.md`.
